Road Rules • सडक नियम

Nepal Road Rules

Essential traffic regulations you must know to drive legally and safely in Nepal

◀

Drive on the Left Side

Nepal follows left-hand traffic. Always keep to the left side of the road.

🔒

Seat Belts Are Mandatory

All occupants must wear seat belts. Fine applies for non-compliance.

⛑

Helmet Required for Motorcycles

Both rider and pillion passenger must wear a proper certified helmet.

📵

No Mobile Phone While Driving

Using a mobile phone while driving (except hands-free) is illegal in Nepal.

🍺

Blood Alcohol Limit: 0.03%

Legal BAC limit is 0.03%. Driving under influence carries serious penalties.

🪪

Valid Licence Required

Always carry a valid driving licence. IDP is accepted for foreigners.

🚦

Priority at Junctions

Give way to vehicles on the right. Always follow traffic police signals above all signs.

📣

Horn Usage

Horns are used frequently in Nepal to warn others, especially on mountain roads.

Mountain Roads • पहाडी सडक

Mountain Road Rules

Nepal's unique mountain highways require special knowledge and extra care

⬆

Uphill Vehicles Have Right of Way

On single-lane mountain roads, vehicles going uphill have priority. Downhill must pull aside.

📣

Horn Before Blind Corners

Always sound your horn before blind corners on mountain roads. Legally required.

⚙

Use Low Gear on Descents

On steep descents, use engine braking (lower gear) instead of continuous braking.

⇔

Uphill Vehicle Stays Center

On very narrow roads, uphill vehicle stays inside (mountain side), downhill moves to outer edge.

🌙

Extra Caution at Night

Avoid mountain roads at night if possible. Use low beam in fog, go slow.

🚫

Do Not Stop on Narrow Roads

Never stop on a narrow mountain road. If you must stop, pull left, use hazard lights.
